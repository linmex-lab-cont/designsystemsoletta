## Quick orientation
- **Brand essence:** *Calma con Criterio* — serene and intelligent. Premium-sober, warm-rational, nautical as structural code. Never beach-cliché, hard-sell, or saturated turquoise.
- **Foundations:** link `styles.css` for all tokens. Marine `#2274A5`, coral accent `#F56B43` (sparingly), ink `#1F2938`, sand neutrals. Display serif = Saudagar (official, self-hosted); body = Barlow.
- **Components:** load `_ds_bundle.js`, read from `window.SolettaDesignSystem_19cf15` — `Logo, Button, Badge, Stat, Card, FeatureBadge, Input`.
- **Patterns to reuse:** sunburst + serif wordmark; coral headline band; circular marine icon discs; big figure + outlined-pill labels for financing; wide-tracked uppercase eyebrows; credential seals as proof.
- **Voice:** Spanish (MX), informal "tú", concrete attributable claims, calm CTAs ("Contáctame"). No emoji.
- See `readme.md` → CONTENT FUNDAMENTALS, VISUAL FOUNDATIONS, ICONOGRAPHY for the full rules, and `ui_kits/website/` + `templates/social-post/` for worked examples.
