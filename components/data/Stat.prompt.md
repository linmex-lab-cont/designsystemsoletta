The signature figure display used across financing plans and feature highlights.

```jsx
<Stat value="50%" label="De enganche" />
<Stat value="24%" label="Plusvalía en Soletta Chicxulub" labelStyle="plain" />
<Stat caption="Medidas desde" value="8 × 20 m" labelStyle="plain" color="ink" />
<Stat value="90%" label="En el primer pago" color="coral" />
```

- `labelStyle="pill"` draws the outlined capsule (financing plans); `plain` is a quiet caption.
- `caption` puts a small uppercase line above the figure ("ACCESO AL MAR" → "a solo 90 m").
- Use `color="white"` over photography.
