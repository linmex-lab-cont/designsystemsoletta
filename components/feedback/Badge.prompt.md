Wide-tracked uppercase label pill — for plan names, section labels, scarcity tags and sub-brand chips.

```jsx
<Badge variant="marine">Plan Inversionista Estratégico</Badge>
<Badge variant="coral" size="lg">Solo 35 lotes disponibles</Badge>
<Badge variant="outline-light">Sisal · Yucatán</Badge>
```

- `outline-light` is for placing over photography. `sand`/`soft` are quiet neutral tags.
- Keep label text short; this is a tag, not a sentence.
