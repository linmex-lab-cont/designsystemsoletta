A sober content surface. White with a hairline border and quiet shadow by default; `sand` and `marine` for warm and dark sections; `media` for a lot/property card.

```jsx
<Card><h3>Escrituración inmediata</h3><p>Certeza jurídica desde el día uno.</p></Card>
<Card variant="marine"><Stat value="24%" label="Plusvalía" color="white" /></Card>
<Card media="lot.jpg" interactive><h3>Lote 12 · 154 m²</h3></Card>
```

- Keep cards airy — generous padding, one idea per card. Restraint over ornament.
- `interactive` adds a calm hover lift; use only for clickable cards.
